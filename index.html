Our Key Initiatives for Coastal Communities

We focus on three essential initiatives to uplift coastal communities. We train local journalists to report accurately on climate issues, and we work to counter misinformation so the narratives about climate change are credible and effective.

01

Training Local Journalists for Impact

We give journalists the skills and resources to report accurately on climate issues.

02

Combating Misinformation in Climate Reporting

We work to keep climate narratives accurate and credible.

03

Fostering Informed Dialogue and Understanding

We create platforms for meaningful conversation on climate.
